# KKL Jinning C04 Page 7 Massing V1.1

This editable GLB is a conceptual site massing based on the historical C04 PDF page 7 plan. The user-provided photos of physical Kinmen distillery models informed the industrial roof forms, tank clusters, planting, and overall model-like appearance.

The user confirmed that the S-shaped feature at the left visitor area is a watercourse connecting the Welcome Fountain (迎賓水池) and Baoyue Xinquan (寶月新泉). V1.1 models it as a continuous blue-green channel with stone edging.

## Included

- `KKL_Jinning_C04_p07_massing_v1.1.glb.gz`: gzip-compressed, editable glTF Binary model. Decompress with `gzip -dc` to obtain a standard `.glb`.
- `preview.jpg`: static isometric preview.
- `metadata.json`: scale-bar conversion, source notes, modeled features, and limitations.

## Important limits

- The plan is historical EIA design material, not a verified as-built survey.
- Building positions are simplified manual digitization from C04 page 7. They are not georeferenced or independently matched to every source vector.
- Building heights, tank counts/details, roof forms, and landscaping are illustrative and informed by the physical-model photos; they are not measured construction data.
- The S-shaped watercourse is modeled as a smooth ribbon between the two confirmed water features. Its smoothness is a visual interpretation, not a surveyed edge.
- Source PDF, extracted source vectors, photographs, and source-image overlays are not included in this public repository.
