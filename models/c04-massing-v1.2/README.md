# KKL Jinning C04 Page 7 Massing V1.2

A conceptual, editable GLB based on the historical C04 page 7 plan. The user-provided physical-model photographs informed the roof palette, industrial tank clusters, visitor landscape, and overall model-diorama treatment.

The S-shaped blue-green channel represents the user-confirmed watercourse connecting the Welcome Fountain (迎賓水池) and Baoyue Xinquan (寶月新泉).

## Files

- `KKL_Jinning_C04_p07_massing_v1.2.glb.gz`: gzip-compressed GLB. Decompress with `gzip -dc` to obtain an editable glTF Binary model.
- `preview.jpg`: default isometric preview with surrounding context.
- `render-context-isometric.jpg`: full-site environmental render.
- `render-visitor-landscape.jpg`: visitor-water-feature close render.
- `metadata.json`: source, scale conversion, inventory, status, and limitations.

## Limits

C04 is historical planning material, not a verified as-built survey. Building footprints are simplified manual digitizations; heights and building/tank details are illustrative. The surrounding terrain, perimeter roads, and tree rows were added for rendering context only. They are not surveyed, georeferenced, or confirmed as present-day site features. The S-shaped watercourse identity and its two endpoints were confirmed by the user; its exact bank geometry and width remain illustrative.

The original PDF, full extracted vectors, and source-image overlays are excluded from this public bundle.
